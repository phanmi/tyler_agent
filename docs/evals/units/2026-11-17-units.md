# Units eval, November 2026

[Eval harness](../../../agent-python/evals/README.md) · [Generated report, default models](2026-11-17-units-default-models-report.md) · [Generated report, low-cost models](2026-11-17-units-low-cost-models-report.md) · [Run before this change](../nutrition-sources/2026-11-16-nutrition-sources.md)

**This run measures [#104](https://github.com/phanmi/tyler_agent/issues/104).** With that change, the agent saves the amount the user gives in the user's own word for its unit, such as cup, slice, scoop or medium. It does not convert the amount to grams or millilitres, and does not add a conversion such as "cup (240 ml)" to the unit. When the user gives two units, such as "one 2 oz bar", either is accepted. Food verification rejects a converted or annotated unit, and its correction step keeps the user's unit; see [Amounts and units](../../architecture/AGENT_OPERATIONS.md#amounts-and-units).

The default models ran the four unit categories. The low-cost models ran those and `many-items`, and are compared with their [run of 2026-11-16](../nutrition-sources/2026-11-16-nutrition-sources.md) on the code before this change.

## Summary

- **Units are kept.**
  - On the 38 cases both low-cost runs share, chats where a record changed the user's amount or unit, or held the whole meal, fell from 73 to 6 over the three models and both modes.
  - The default models changed no unit in any of their 288 chats.
  - No model changed a unit in any of the 180 chats of the new `uncommon-unit` category.
- **No record held the whole meal.** Such records, with a unit such as "meal" or "snack" and the calories of the whole message, were in 19 chats before and in none after.
- **With verification on, fully correct chats rose on all three low-cost models.**
  - `gpt-6-luna`: from 82.5% to 92.1%.
  - `claude-haiku-5-5`: from 74.6% to 91.2%.
  - `deepseek-flash`: from 78.1% to 82.5%.
  - On messages that list many foods, these are each model's best results in four runs.
- **With verification off, wrong dates rose sharply on two models.**
  - `gpt-6-luna` saved a wrong date in 24 chats against 2, and `deepseek-flash` in 28 against 10.
  - So their fully correct chats fell, from 81.6% to 68.4% and from 79.8% to 66.7%. Not counting dates, they rose slightly.
  - Nothing in this change is about dates, but with verification off it is the only difference the chat model sees, so it may be the cause. See [Wrong dates with verification off](#wrong-dates-with-verification-off).
- **`claude-haiku-5-5` improved with verification off too,** from 77.2% to 89.5%.
- **The scoop of protein powder missed in every chat.** All five models saved 120 kcal, against 91.5 kcal for FoodData Central's 26 g scoop. The other `uncommon-unit` misses were nutrition values for corn and tuna, and dates. The scoop case now names a brand, and the Pizza Hut case is gone; see [Cases changed after the run](#cases-changed-after-the-run).
- **One unit is still converted often.** `deepseek-flash` saved a grande latte as 16 fl oz in 5 of 6 chats.
- **The two runs cost about $5.33** by the reports' estimates.

Both generated reports show version 0.2.1, the release version in the build configuration; the code is newer than that release.

A chat is **fully correct** when every food the case expects is saved with the right name, amount, unit, date and nutrition, and nothing else is saved. Calories pass within 20% or 15 kcal of the reference, whichever is wider, and protein, carbs, fat and fiber within 20% or 2 g. The [harness README](../../../agent-python/evals/README.md#read-the-report) defines every measure.

The unit must now be the user's own word for it. A converted unit, such as 16 fl oz for a grande, is wrong, and so is a unit with a conversion added, such as "bar (68 g)". When the user gives two units, as in "one regular 2 oz Snickers bar", 1 bar and 2 oz are both right, but a mix such as 2 bars is not. The README's [Accepted units](../../../agent-python/evals/README.md#accepted-units) has the rules.

**The run before was scored with the old units.**

- It did not accept 2 oz for the Snickers bar. In the counts below, its Snickers chats saved as 2 oz count as correct, as they would now: 5 `gpt-6-luna` chats, 6 `claude-haiku-5-5` chats and 2 `deepseek-flash` chats.
- It accepted generic words such as "serving" or "cup" for the Snickers bar, the medium fries and the grande latte. A chat that saved one of those words was correct then and would be wrong now. The generated report lists only chats that were not fully correct, so these cannot be counted.

## Units

### Low-cost models

**Before the change**, the records that changed a unit were mostly conversions and annotations:

- A grande latte as 16 fl oz or "grande (16 fl oz)".
- Medium fries as 111 or 117 g, or "serving (medium, 114 g)".
- A Clif Bar as "bar (68 g)" or 68 g.
- A medium potato as 173 g, a cup of milk as 244 g, and corn tortillas as 78 g.
- A medium tomato or bell pepper as "medium tomato (about 123 g)" or "medium piece".
- Two tablespoons of peanut butter as one "serving", and whole meals as "meal" or "snack".

**After the change**, only two kinds remain:

- `deepseek-flash` saved the grande latte as 16 fl oz or 16 oz in 5 of 6 chats, 3 with verification off and 2 with it on. It did the same in all 6 chats before. With verification on, the verifier accepted 16 fl oz twice; in the third chat the latte was not saved.
- `gpt-6-luna` saved the Snickers bar as 1 "2 oz bar" in 1 chat with verification off. The harness scores this as wrong because it mixes the two units, though it is close to the user's own words.

### Default models

The default models changed no unit in any of their 288 chats.

The only earlier run of these models is the [first eval](../eval-harness/2026-11-12-food-recording.md) of 2026-11-12. That run used older code, from before #105, and older references. On the 18 `amount-and-unit`, `household-unit` and `branded` cases, it had these unit misses:

- **`gpt-6-sol`:** the Snickers bar as 2 oz in 6 chats, which is right under the new rule, and medium fries as "medium order" in 1.
- **`claude-sonnet-5-5`:** the Snickers bar as 57 g in 6 chats, a cup of milk as 244 ml or "cup (240 ml)" in 4, and 6 oz of chicken as 170 g in 1.

## Wrong dates with verification off

- **What happened.**
  - With verification off, `gpt-6-luna` saved a wrong date in 24 of 114 chats, against 2 before. `deepseek-flash` did so in 28 against 10.
  - Both rose in every category.
  - `claude-haiku-5-5` and the default models saved no wrong date.
- **The dates were made up.** Over all 44 cases, `gpt-6-luna` saved 27 different dates in 29 chats, from 2025-01-14 to 2026-11-03. `deepseek-flash` saved 2026-09-07 in 32 of its 35 chats with a wrong date.
- **How this compares with earlier runs.** In the two runs of all 62 cases on 2026-11-16, `gpt-6-luna` saved a wrong date in 4 and 7 of 186 chats, and `deepseek-flash` in 31 and 19. So `deepseek-flash`'s count is near what it has done before, and `gpt-6-luna`'s is far above it.
- **Why it may happen.**
  - A model learns today's date only by calling `getCurrentTime`.
  - `recordFood`'s date field does not say to do that, and `getCurrentTime`'s description says to use it "when the user asks what time it is now".
  - With verification off, the chat model sees the same prompts and tools in both runs except for `recordFood`'s longer description. So the change may make these two models skip the lookup more often, or the providers' models may have behaved differently that day. One run of each cannot tell, and the report keeps no transcripts.
- **Verification hides it.** With verification on, the verifier sees today's date and rejects a wrong one. So only 0 and 1 chats had a wrong date.
- **The verifier did more correcting.** With verification on, records saved after a rejection were 0.48 per chat on `gpt-6-luna` and 0.62 on `deepseek-flash`, against 0.14 and 0.24 before. On `claude-haiku-5-5` they fell from 0.11 to 0.05.
  - The runs cover different cases, so this is only a rough comparison.
  - Correcting wrong dates fits these numbers, but the report keeps no rejection reasons.
- **Foods were saved twice.** In 10 chats with verification off, a food the case expects was saved twice: 4 on `gpt-6-luna` and 6 on `deepseek-flash`.
  - In 9 of them, every food in the message was saved twice.
  - The run before had none on these cases.
  - The report does not show whether the second save followed a date lookup.

## What else got worse

- **`gpt-6-luna` missed more nutrition values.**
  - Its chats with a value outside the tolerance rose from 4 to 12 with verification off and from 3 to 6 with it on.
  - The misses are spread over several foods. Two slices of bread were saved at 202 kcal against 161.3, in 2 chats; half a cup of marinara sauce at 82 kcal against 66.0; ground beef with too much fat; cranberry sauce with too few calories; and chocolate with too many carbs.
  - `claude-haiku-5-5` went from 15 to 12 off and stayed at 10 on. `deepseek-flash` went from 6 to 4 in both modes.
  - One run cannot show whether estimating by the cup or slice, rather than by the gram, makes `gpt-6-luna`'s values worse.
- **Each model call carries a longer tool description.** `recordFood`'s description grew from 572 to 948 characters. Input tokens per chat cannot be compared with the run before, because the case mix differs.

## Cost

- $3.00 for `claude-sonnet-5-5`.

The providers' bills were not checked against these. Both runs were outside DeepSeek's 06:00 to 10:00 UTC peak hours, so the report's off-peak price applies.

## Cases changed after the run

Neither change below has been run.

- **The `pizza-hut-slices` case was removed.** Pizza crusts, sizes and toppings vary too much for one reference value to be fair. In this run the case was not fully correct in all 6 `gpt-6-sol` chats, 6 `gpt-6-luna` chats and 4 `deepseek-flash` chats. In the run before, it was not fully correct in 6 `gpt-6-luna` chats and 4 `deepseek-flash` chats.
- **The generic scoop case was replaced by a `branded` case.** The new case is 1 scoop of Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Chocolate. Its values come from Dymatize's US page: 120 kcal, 25 g of protein, 2 g of carbs and 1 g of fat for a 32 g scoop. The page gives fiber only as "<1 g", so fiber is not checked. A scoop's weight depends on the brand, so the case names one. `uncommon-unit` now has 5 cases.

## Limits of this run

- **One run each.** On 114 chats, one chat is worth about 0.9 points, so a change of a few chats is within noise.
- **The run before was scored with the old units.** Its Snickers chats were rescored by hand. Its fries and latte chats saved with a generic word cannot be, because the report lists only chats that were not fully correct.
- **The default models have no run of the code before this change.** The comparison with the first eval spans other changes as well.
- **Why the dates went wrong is not shown.** The report keeps no transcripts or rejection reasons. The two runs were also 13 hours apart, so a change on the providers' side is not ruled out.
- **The `uncommon-unit` cases use only units FoodData Central gives a weight for.** So the category has no unit such as a glass of milk.
- **Replies are not kept.** The report shows what was saved, not what the agent told the user about a food it did not save.
